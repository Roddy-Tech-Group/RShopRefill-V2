## Security measures

- Passwords and transaction PINs stored as one-way hashes; two-factor secrets and the closed-account identity register encrypted at rest.
- Access to customer records restricted to authenticated admin roles; every administrative change to sensitive fields is written to the audit log with the actor.
- Financial tables reject deletion of a customer row at the database level; accounts are closed by anonymisation, never deleted.
- Data export requires a one-time code to the account email; downloads are rate limited and audited.
- Transport encryption (HTTPS) on every endpoint; daily encrypted database backups with retention managed by `backup:clean`.

## Data subject rights

- Access: self-serve PDF export from account settings, or by email to the DPO (see `docs/runbooks/data-protection.md`).
- Correction: account settings, or by email to the DPO.
- Deletion: self-serve account closure with a 30-day grace period, or by email to the DPO (actioned from the admin panel, audited). Records required by financial and anti-money-laundering law are retained for the windows above and then removed.
- Objection and withdrawal of consent: communication preferences in account settings; every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link.
